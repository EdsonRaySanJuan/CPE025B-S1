About Us

OrgLink is a web-based platform designed to serve as an official information hub for an organization or student club. It provides members and the public with easy access to organizational details, officers, events, and membership registration.

Home

Welcome to OrgLink! Our platform is dedicated to keeping you informed about our organization's activities, leadership, and opportunities to get involved. Whether you're a current member or interested in joining, you'll find all the information you need right here.

Officers

  • President Oversees the overall direction of the organization and leads major activities and meetings.
  • Vice President Assists the president and coordinates internal organizational affairs.
  • Secretary Manages documentation, meeting minutes, and official records.
  • Treasurer Handles financial matters, budgeting, and funds management.
  • Committee Heads Lead specific committees such as Events, Membership, and Communications.
  • Active Members A list of registered members who actively participate in organizational activities and programs.
