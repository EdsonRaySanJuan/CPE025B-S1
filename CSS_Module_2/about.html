About me

I’m a 3rd year Computer Engineering student at the Technological Institute of the Philippines, specializing in Human–AI Interaction.

I enjoy exploring how people think, feel, and behave around intelligent systems, then using that understanding to build interfaces that feel natural, helpful, and a little bit fun.

Think of this space as my lab: I build things, break them, learn, and share what I discover along the way.

I care about building technology that actually helps people, not just impresses them. As I grow in this field, I want to collaborate with others who are curious about AI, design, and engineering.
