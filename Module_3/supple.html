Chase Atlantic's Album

Chase Atlantic is an Australian band known for their unique blend of R&B, pop, and rock. Their self-titled album "Chase Atlantic" was released in 2017 and features a mix of moody, atmospheric tracks that showcase their signature sound.

Mitchel Cave – Main Vocalist/Singer
Christian Anthony – Vocals/Guitar
Clinton Cave – Saxophonist/Producer
Jesse Boyle – Drummer
Pat Wilde – Lead Guitarist
