Our Services

Core treatment services

  • Withdrawal management / medical detox with 24‑hour nursing and physician oversight for people in acute distress from alcohol or drug use.
  • Short‑term residential treatment (about 28 days) with structured daily programming, education on chemical dependency, relapse‑prevention skills, and on‑site drug testing.
  • Longer‑term residential services (3.1 level of care) that combine counseling, life‑skills training, and a structured living environment to support early recovery.

Outpatient and continuing care

  • Intensive outpatient treatment (IOP) for alcohol, drug, and gambling addiction, usually 8–16 weeks of cognitive‑based group sessions several times per week.
  • Regular outpatient counseling and ongoing aftercare to help clients maintain sobriety and transition back into work, housing, and community life.

Supportive and wraparound services

  • Case management to link clients with housing, employment, healthcare, and other community resources.
  • Family education and therapy, on‑site 12‑step meetings, and spiritual support consistent with The Salvation Army’s Christian mission.
  • Health education, HIV testing and education, recreational activities, and access to an on‑site computer lab to build life and job skills.

Who they serve

  • Adult men and women, including people with co‑occurring mental health issues or a history of trauma, who are motivated for treatment.
  • Primarily residents of Central Indiana, though admissions can come from anywhere in the state.
