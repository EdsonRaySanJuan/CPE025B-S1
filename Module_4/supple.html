About St. John-Hill Academy

SJHA began as a tutorial service for neighborhood children. In 1996, it affiliated with the National Federation of Women's Club of the Philippines (N.F.W.C.). As the community grew, the school expanded from Pre-school to Grade 4, eventually completing both Elementary and Secondary levels.

Today, SJHA follows the DepEd K to 12 Basic Education Curriculum, preparing learners for employment after Senior High School and equipping them with globally competitive skills.

Our Mission

St. John-Hill Academy offers an enriched learning environment that fosters growth, development, and confidence. Our curriculum and teaching methods empower students to take the next step in their education and embrace the future with assurance.

Inspired by St. John the Baptizer, our institution promotes prophetic leadership, moral integrity, and spiritual awareness — guiding learners to live with purpose and righteousness.

Our Community

We are proud to teach bright and curious students from Morong and surrounding areas. If you're new to our community and want to learn more about our values, academics, or staff, we welcome you to reach out and connect.

Our Reputation

St. John-Hill Academy is the only private school in eastern Rizal known for delivering quality education. It is a consistent top-performing school in the Province of Rizal and Region IV-A (CALABARZON), and proudly recognized as an English-speaking institution.
