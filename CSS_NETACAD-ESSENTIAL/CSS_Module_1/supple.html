Organization Background

OrgLink was founded in 2024 by student leaders who wanted to create a platform that promotes collaboration, leadership development, and service.

Mission

To connect students through collaborative initiatives, enhance leadership skills, and encourage active participation in academic and community activities.

Vision

To become a unified and empowered student organization that inspires positive change within and beyond the campus.
